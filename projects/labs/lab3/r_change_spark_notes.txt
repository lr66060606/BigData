spakr-submit errors mean it could be trying to use a broken install of spark.



# Check your installed versions of spark:
library(sparklyr)
sparklyr::spark_installed_versions()
sparklyr::spark_home_dir()
list.files(file.path(sparklyr::spark_home_dir(), "bin"))


Your output may be:
> sparklyr::spark_installed_versions() 
spark hadoop dir 
1 4.0.1 2.7 C:\\Users\\andyrox\\AppData\\Local/spark/spark-4.0.1-bin-hadoop2.7 
2 4.0.1 3 C:\\Users\\andyrox\\AppData\\Local/spark/spark-4.0.1-bin-hadoop3 
> sparklyr::spark_home_dir()
 [1] "C:\\Users\\andyrox\\AppData\\Local/spark/spark-4.0.1-bin-hadoop2.7" 
> list.files(file.path(sparklyr::spark_home_dir(), "bin"))
 character(0)

This means that sparklyr is defaulting to a broken spark install.

use this to uninstall:

library(sparklyr)
spark_uninstall(version = "4.0.1", hadoop_version = "2.7")

# you should see:
# spark-4.0.1-bin-hadoop2.7 successfully uninstalled.



------- MANUAL REMOVAL (if spark was not installed via sparklyr) ----------

Step 1 — Delete the bad Spark folder
unlink("C:/Users/andyrox/AppData/Local/spark/spark-4.0.1-bin-hadoop2.7", recursive = TRUE, force = TRUE)

Then verify it’s gone:
sparklyr::spark_installed_versions()

You should now only see the hadoop3 entry:
  spark hadoop  dir
1 4.0.1      3  C:\Users\andyrox\AppData\Local\spark\spark-4.0.1-bin-hadoop3
